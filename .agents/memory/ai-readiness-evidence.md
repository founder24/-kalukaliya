---
name: AI readiness review evidence
description: Keep internal page checks distinct from authoritative curriculum verification in SEO, GEO, and AEO reporting.
---

For Syrabit SEO/GEO/AEO reviews, distinguish page-internal support from official curriculum accuracy. A rendered page can be scored for whether its visible text supports its own claims, but this does not establish that those claims match an official textbook or syllabus.

**Why:** Readiness reviews can otherwise turn an internal consistency check into an unsupported claim of curriculum verification or a misleading perfect score.

**How to apply:** Record the source scope and reviewer type with every manual evidence set. Keep the composite N/A when required field, crawler, or authoritative source evidence is unavailable; resolve reviewer disagreements conservatively and preserve both original decisions.
