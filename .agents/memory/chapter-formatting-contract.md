---
name: Chapter formatting contract
description: User-approved formatting rules for future Syrabit educational chapter notes
---

Future chapter formatting should preserve complete source content while imposing a semantic Markdown hierarchy: one chapter title, major topics, subtopics, compact related paragraphs, ordered derivations, meaningful lists/tables, and restrained callouts. Keep each topic with its notes, separate major topic blocks in the reader, reduce gaps within one subtopic, and use calm emphasis rather than saturated colors.

**Why:** The user confirmed this reading treatment is comfortable and easier to study than dense plain text or highly colorful cards.

**How to apply:** Use the shared formatter prompt before publishing other chapters. Never summarize, invent formulas, add decorative rules between a heading and its notes, or treat every sentence as a separate paragraph.
