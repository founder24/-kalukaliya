---
name: Syllabus PDF alignment
description: Safe rules for mapping stored official syllabus documents to subjects and chapters.
---

Stored syllabus documents are authoritative for curriculum alignment, but their
catalog records may have only board-level hierarchy IDs and no subject, class,
stream, or course IDs. Never attach them to subjects using extracted-body
keyword matches alone.

**Why:** Generic words in syllabus prose produced convincing but false matches,
including Artificial Intelligence to Education and Costing and Taxation to
History. Such matches would silently corrupt chapter names and content.

**How to apply:** Require an unambiguous official filename/title, matching
programme and stream, plus a parsed unit/chapter table. Back up and archive
confirmed out-of-syllabus rows; do not reuse their notes unless provenance is
also confirmed official.
