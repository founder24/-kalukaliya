---
name: AdSense finalization boundary
description: Google AdSense performance reports contain estimates; finalized earnings are posted after month-end in Payments and transaction history.
---

Treat AdSense performance-report earnings as estimates, not finalized provider receipts. Google states that finalized earnings are posted to Payments and transaction history after month-end and may differ because of invalid-activity adjustments and billing-time-zone cutoffs. A finalized monthly total cannot be silently attributed to individual referral weeks.

**Why:** Referral rewards use finalized provider revenue, and importing estimates or inventing a weekly split could overstate available funding.

**How to apply:** Before connecting a revenue importer, verify the approved source supplies final values for the exact period the ledger requires. If AdSense only supplies finalized monthly totals, keep weekly imports disabled unless the owner explicitly approves an auditable monthly-to-week allocation policy and corresponding ledger changes.
