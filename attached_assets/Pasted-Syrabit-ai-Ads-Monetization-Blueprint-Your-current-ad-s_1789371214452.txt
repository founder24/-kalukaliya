2. Adcash — primary network

Formats you planned

Format	Purpose	Placement

🎥 Video Slider	High-value video monetization	Within content/scroll

Adcash officially supports these formats, including video slider/pre-roll, banners, interstitials and in-page push.

7. Interstitial strategy

Don't trigger an interstitial every few minutes.

Use it around natural transitions.

For example:

Chapter 1
   ↓
Questions
   ↓
[ INTERSTITIAL ]
   ↓
PYQs

or:

Subject A
   ↓
[ INTERSTITIAL ]
   ↓
Subject B

The student should perceive it as a navigation transition, not an interruption during studying.

9. Video opportunity model

Your earlier concept was:

Full subject journey

If a student goes through:

5 Notes pages
+
5 Questions pages
+
5 PYQ pages

and you use:

Notes:     3 video opportunities × 5 = 15
Questions: 2 × 5 = 10
PYQs:      3 × 5 = 15

then:

40 potential video opportunities

But this is not 40 guaranteed video impressions.

The distinction is crucial:

> Opportunity ≠ impression ≠ filled impression ≠ monetized impression.



Actual revenue depends on fill rate, viewability, user behavior, frequency caps, network demand and geography.

11. Target impression model

For planning, we previously used:

Strong-engagement scenario

~40 actual monetized impressions/user/day

But don't assume every student generates this.

A more useful planning framework is:

Engagement	Actual impressions/day

Light	10–15
Moderate	20–30
Strong	30–40
Very heavy	40+


Your ₹2/user/day target requires approximately:

₹50 blended eCPM × 40 impressions = ₹2/user/day

So ₹2/user/day is an optimization target, not a guaranteed Assam traffic rate.


---

12. Blended eCPM model

Don't add individual format eCPMs together.

Instead calculate:

Total ad revenue
──────────────────── × 1,000
Total monetized impressions

For Assam-heavy mobile educational traffic, our planning bands were:

Blended eCPM	Assessment

₹20–25	Conservative
₹30–40	Realistic planning range
₹45–55	Strong
₹55–70	Very strong
₹70+	Excellent/upside


I'd use ₹40 blended eCPM as the initial business-planning base case, then replace it with your actual measured number once Syrabit has sufficient traffic.

The design principle

High-value ads on long pages, lighter ads on short pages, interstitials only at natural transitions, and persistent native/social monetization without covering educational content.

That gives you the best balance between ad revenue, student retention, and the ₹1 campus-acquisition model.
