5. Visitor identity

Do not use IP address as the sole unique-user identifier.

Use a combination of:

First-party referral cookie
        +
session/user identifier
        +
IP/network signal
        +
browser/device signals
        +
timestamp
        +
fraud signals

For logged-in Syrabit students, the strongest identifier should be:

Syrabit user_id

This gives you much better deduplication.

6. Attribution rule

Recommended rule:

> First eligible promoter gets attribution for that visitor during the weekly attribution window.



Example:

Monday

Student X
   ↓
CL001 link
   ↓
Attributed → CL001

Later:

Student X
   ↓
CL002 link
   ↓
Already attributed
   ↓
CL002 gets NO credit

This prevents promoter competition over the same visitor.

7. Weekly attribution

Use a weekly period:

Monday 00:00
       ↓
     Sunday

       ↓

You need historical records for:

8. Verified visitor event

Create an event such as:

verified_referral_visit

A visitor becomes verified only after your validation rules are satisfied.

Example:

Referral click
      ↓
Page successfully loaded
      ↓
Engagement threshold
      ↓
Not duplicate
      ↓
Not bot/fraud
      ↓
VERIFIED

The exact engagement threshold should be configurable rather than hard-coded.

For example:

minimum_session_seconds = 30

You can later change it to 45/60 seconds without rebuilding the system.

11. Trial tier logic

New lead:

tier = TRIAL
rate = ₹1
weekly_cap = ₹100
unlock_target = 500

Therefore:

visitors <= 100
      ↓
₹1 × visitors

visitors > 100
      ↓
₹100 maximum


---

12. Unlock logic

If:

verified_visitors >= 500

during a single weekly period:

tier → FULL

The Full Tier should then be permanently unlocked, unless you later introduce a separate quality/suspension policy.

Importantly:

> The weekly counter resets, but the tier does not.

21. Example at scale

Suppose:

10 campus leads

each generate:

500 verified visitors/week

Then:

10 × 500
= 5,000 verified visitors/week

Referral payout:

5,000 × ₹1
= ₹5,000/week

Approximately:

₹20,000/month

if you use four weeks for simple planning.

But those 5,000 students can return repeatedly.

For example, if the cohort averages 15 monetized active days/month and your realized revenue is ₹1.20–₹1.60 per active user-day:

5,000 × 15 × ₹1.20
= ₹90,000

to

5,000 × 15 × ₹1.60
= ₹1,20,000

So the illustrative relationship becomes:

₹5,000 acquisition cost/week → potentially much larger cumulative ad revenue from repeated usage.

23. Most important implementation rule

Never calculate earnings directly from clicks.

Use this hierarchy:

CLICK
  ↓
VISIT
  ↓
ELIGIBLE VISITOR
  ↓
VERIFIED VISITOR
  ↓
PAYABLE VISITOR

Only payable verified visitors affect the ₹1 payout.

That protects Syrabit from bot traffic and protects you financially as the referral network grows.
