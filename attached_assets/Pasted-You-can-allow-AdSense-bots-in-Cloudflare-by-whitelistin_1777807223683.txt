You can allow AdSense bots in Cloudflare by **whitelisting their User‑Agents** (and optionally IPs) in **Firewall Rules** so they’re not blocked or challenged. [clashpanda](https://clashpanda.com/allow-google-adsense-bots-with-cloudflare-for-higher-rpm/)

***

### 1. **Create Firewall Rules in Cloudflare**

- Go to **Cloudflare Dashboard → Firewall → Firewall Rules → Create a Firewall Rule**.  
- For **each AdSense bot**, set:

  **Rule 1 – Mediapartners‑Google**  
  - **Field**: `User Agent`  
  - **Operator**: `contains`  
  - **Value**: `Mediapartners-Google`  
  - **Action**: `Allow`  

  **Rule 2 – Google‑Display‑Ads‑Bot**  
  - Field: `User Agent`  
  - Operator: `contains`  
  - Value: `Google-Display-Ads-Bot`  
  - Action: `Allow`  

 [clashpanda](https://clashpanda.com/optimal-interaction-between-your-site-and-adsense-bots/)

You can also add **IP‑based allow rules** if you want to be extra safe, but User‑Agent rules are usually enough. [clashpanda](https://clashpanda.com/optimal-interaction-between-your-site-and-adsense-bots/)

***

### 2. **Tune Security / Bot Settings**

- **Security Level**:  
  - Keep it **Medium** or **Low** for pages showing AdSense, or at least **don’t challenge** the AdSense‑bot rules above. [clashpanda](https://clashpanda.com/allow-google-adsense-bots-with-cloudflare-for-higher-rpm/)
- **Bot Fight Mode**:  
  - If you use it, **don’t block** those AdSense‑bot User‑Agents; treat them as “good bots”. [developers.cloudflare](https://developers.cloudflare.com/waf/custom-rules/use-cases/allow-traffic-from-verified-bots/)

***

### 3. **Check robots.txt as well**

In your `robots.txt`, avoid blocking these:

```
User-agent: Mediapartners-Google
Allow: /

User-agent: Google-Display-Ads-Bot
Allow: /
```

This helps AdSense bots crawl and verify your pages. [clashpanda](https://clashpanda.com/allow-google-adsense-bots-with-cloudflare-for-higher-rpm/)

***

### 4. **Verify the setup**

- Use **Google AdSense Crawler Tester** (in AdSense → Sites → “Test ad tag”) to confirm AdSense bots can access your notes/PYQ pages. [support.google](https://support.google.com/adsense/thread/176833585/cloudflare-google-adsense?hl=en)
