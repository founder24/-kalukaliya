## **📊 REVENUE-OPTIMIZED PLACEMENT BLUEPRINT**

### **1. PYQs (PREVIOUS YEAR QUESTIONS) - HIGHEST REVENUE SECTION**
*Why: Students spend 15-30 minutes here during exam prep*

**Ad Placements (Per Paper):**

**A. Top Banner (Above Page 1)**
- **Format:** 320x50 Mobile Leaderboard
- **Position:** Directly above the first question paper image
- **Revenue Impact:** High viewability, non-intrusive

**B. Between Pages (Page 1 → Page 2, Page 2 → Page 3, etc.)**
- **Format:** 320x100 Large Mobile Banner or Responsive Display
- **Position:** Between each image with 20px buffer zones
- **Frequency:** Every 2-3 pages (don't overdo it)
- **Revenue Impact:** ⭐ **HIGHEST** - Natural scroll pause point

**C. Sticky Anchor Ad (Global)**
- **Format:** Auto Ads Anchor (Sticky Bottom)
- **Position:** Fixed at bottom of screen
- **Revenue Impact:** 100% viewability throughout the session

**D. Vignette Ad (Between Page Loads)**
- **Format:** Auto Ads Vignette (Full-screen)
- **Trigger:** When clicking from PYQ list to open a paper
- **Frequency Cap:** 1 per 10 minutes
- **Revenue Impact:** Very High CPM

---

### **2. NOTES SECTION - HIGH REVENUE**
*Why: Long-form content with 3-5 minute dwell time*

**Ad Placements (Per Notes Page):**

**A. First In-Article Ad**
- **Position:** After **2nd paragraph** (below introduction)
- **Format:** In-Article Ad (Responsive)
- **Revenue Impact:** ⭐ **VERY HIGH** - Above the fold

**B. Mid-Content Ads**
- **Position:** After every **3rd or 4th paragraph**
- **Format:** In-Article Ad (Responsive)
- **Frequency:** 2-3 ads per long note (1000+ words)
- **Revenue Impact:** High - Natural reading breaks

**C. Before Headings**
- **Position:** Right before H2/H3 subheadings
- **Format:** In-Article Ad
- **Revenue Impact:** Medium-High - Visual break point

**D. End of Content**
- **Position:** After the last paragraph, before "Related Notes"
- **Format:** In-Feed Ad (matches note style)
- **Revenue Impact:** Medium - Final impression

---

### **3. QUESTIONS SECTION - MEDIUM REVENUE**
*Why: Quick answers, but high intent*

**Ad Placements (CRITICAL RULES):**

**✅ DO:**
- Place ad **below the complete answer/solution**
- Place ad **between Question 1 and Question 2** (if multiple on page)
- Place ad **above "Show Answer" button** (if answer is hidden)

**❌ NEVER:**
- Place ad **between Question and Answer** (kills UX)
- Place ad **in the middle of an explanation**

**Optimal Placement:**
```
[Question Text]
[Answer/Solution]
[Explanation]
---
[AD: 300x250 or Responsive]  ← "Reward Zone"
---
[Next Question or Related Questions]
```

```css

    align-items: center;
    background: #f8f9fa;

/* Buffer Zone Between Image and Ad */
```
