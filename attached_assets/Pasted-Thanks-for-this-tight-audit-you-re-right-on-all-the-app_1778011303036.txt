### ✅ **Embedding‑model‑&‑failover‑fix**

- **Primary‑embedding‑space**:  
  - `EmbeddingGemma‑300M` (300‑dim‑Gemma‑class) on **Cloudflare‑Workers‑AI** → **Pinecone‑index‑in‑aws‑ap‑south‑1`.  
- **Embedding‑model‑mismatch‑on‑failover**:  
  - **No‑direct‑index‑mixing**:  
    - When `CF‑/embed`‑is‑down, `synchat‑backend` falls back to **Vertex‑AI‑multilingual‑embeddings** for **new‑chunks‑but‑only‑to‑a‑separate‑"fallback"`‑namespace‑in‑Pinecone**:  
      - `namespace=cached_gemma_today + namespace=fallback_vertex_pending_reembed`.  
    - **In‑flight‑turns** use **Vectorize‑cache‑hits** only; **new‑chunks** are queued for **re‑embed‑when‑Cloudflare‑returns** (via AWS‑Lambda‑job).  
  - **Config**:  
    - `RAG_EMBEDDING_PROVIDER=cf_gemma` / `RAG_EMBEDDING_PROVIDER=fallback_vertex` (with clear‑docs on namespace‑semantics).  
  - This is **availability‑correctness‑trade‑off**:  
    - Availability‑OK;  
    - Correctness‑“good‑enough‑for‑now‑/‑re‑embed‑later” while **no‑index‑corruption**.  

✅ **Result**: You **explicitly accept** **“degraded‑read‑only‑+‑queue‑for‑re‑embed”** during Worker‑outage, not silent‑index‑mix‑corruption.
