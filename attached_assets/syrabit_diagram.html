04 — Four Innovation Pillars That Define Syrabit
🗣️
Vernacular AI
First AI tutor to speak, read, and reason in Assamese. Sarvam AI multilingual models power both chat and voice — the 40+ lakh Assamese-medium students finally have an AI in their language.
📡
Curriculum RAG
Retrieval-Augmented Generation over verified SEBA/AHSEC textbook content. Answers are pulled from the actual book, not hallucinated — every response is curriculum-exact and exam-safe.
🧠
Student Memory
Long-term per-student memory tracks every studied topic, weak area, and past doubt. The tutor gets smarter about each student over time — no competitor offers this for Indian students.
🏗️
Full Content Stack
Notes + Q&A + Previous Year Papers in one bilingual platform with human staff quality control. Eliminates the need for multiple apps, books, and coaching centres.
