Trim replit.md, extract decisions
What & Why
replit.md has grown to ~10k tokens, so the system flags it on every turn and re-loads the entire Architecture-decisions block (~7k tokens of historical task summaries) into the agent's context. Extracting that block into a dedicated docs/architecture/decisions.md and leaving short one-line summaries in replit.md cuts roughly 7k tokens off every future turn while preserving every fact (the long block is moved verbatim, not deleted).

The threat model permits this: the Architecture block contains references and config keys but no secrets, passwords, or staff/admin credentials.
