Yes — and **because most visitors are page‑only browsers**, you can **slim the cost way down** while still **keeping the 30‑chat‑API‑calls‑per‑user‑/‑month‑free** cap.

Here’s how to **minimize cost** under that usage pattern:

***

### 1. **Hard‑budget the 30‑chat‑calls‑per‑user‑per‑month**

- **Enforce** the **30‑turn‑limit** strictly at the **API‑gateway / Edge‑Logic**:
  - Count per‑`user_id`‑per‑month, not‑just‑per‑session‑or‑per‑day.  
- Optionally add **soft‑per‑day‑limit** (e.g., 2–3‑turns‑per‑day) so you avoid **burst‑DDoS‑style‑AI‑calls**.

This keeps **high‑Engagement‑Users** from **exploding** your **GPT‑4.1‑nano** bill.

***

### 2. **Push more traffic to cheap / cached / read‑only paths**

Since most traffic is **browser‑/‑SSR‑/‑PDF‑views**, do:

- **Maximize Cloudflare‑edge‑caching**:
  - **Static‑/‑SSR‑routes** (`/`, `/library`, `/degree`, `/unit`, `/chapter`, `/subject`) should be **long‑TTL‑cached at Cloudflare**.  
  - Use **`Cache‑Reserve`**, **`D1`‑/‑`KV`‑syllabus‑/‑chapter‑metadata‑cache** to avoid hitting DB‑on‑every‑render.  
- **Cache‑PDFs / assets on R2**:
  - Serve all **PDFs‑/‑audio‑/‑exports** via **Cloudflare‑R2‑+‑Cache‑Reserve**, not‑from‑ACA‑or‑Mongo‑on‑every‑request.  
- **Cache‑“heavy‑reads”**:
  - Cache **goal‑summaries**, **model‑papers**, **flashcards** at **Cloudflare‑bytes / D1 / KV**, so you don’t re‑call‑AI‑on‑every‑refresh.

For **page‑visitors**, this turns most of their traffic into **near‑zero‑AI‑cost reads**.

***

### 3. **Push chat‑workload to the cheapest‑reasonable‑secondary models**

While keeping **`gpt‑4.1‑nano`** as the **English‑chat‑primary**, you can:

- **Tightly‑constrain‑tokens‑per‑turn** (e.g., **2k‑3k‑max‑context‑+‑history‑truncation**) → directly‑reduces‑AI‑cost.  
- **Route‑early‑/‑low‑engagement‑chat‑turns** (e.g., <3‑turns‑per‑session) via **Workers‑AI‑Mistral‑7B** or **Qwen‑3‑0.6B** instead of **GPT‑4.1‑nano**, if you can safely **degrade‑UX‑for‑low‑engagement‑users**.  
- **GPT‑4.1‑nano‑only‑on**:
  - **Long‑Q&A**, **exam‑clarification**, **doubt‑resolution**, **high‑retention‑users**.

This keeps **most‑casual‑users‑on‑the‑cheapest‑models**, while **core‑engagement‑users** see the **full‑GPT‑4.1‑nano‑experience**.

***

### 4. **Turn off speculative / admin‑only AI‑jobs that don’t drive core UX**

You already have **GCP‑/‑AWS‑/‑Azure‑AI‑jobs** for:
- **Web‑Risk**, **GSC‑/‑Indexing**, **Exa‑/‑Tavily‑search**, **Web‑Security‑Scanner**, **NLP‑/‑Fact‑Check‑/‑KG‑Search**, **Books‑/‑PageSpeed‑Insights**, **Batch‑Gemini‑RAI‑(...)**.

You can:

- **Reduce frequency** of **non‑core‑batch‑jobs** (e.g., weekly‑instead‑of‑daily‑page‑scan / index‑refresh),  
- **Cancel** any **non‑critical‑batch‑Gemini‑RAI‑/‑NLP‑jobs**,  
- Keep only **core‑batch‑workflows** (e.g., **Google‑Indexing**, **SEO‑fan‑out**, **Web‑Risk‑check‑new‑PYQ‑/‑content**).

***

### 5. **Right‑size and autoscale backend‑resources**

- **Azure‑ACA** can be **small‑/‑medium‑+‑autoscale‑min‑/‑max** for **100k‑DAU‑read‑+‑30‑API‑calls‑per‑user‑per‑month**.  
- **Mongo‑Atlas‑cluster** doesn’t need over‑sizing; right‑size to your **metadata‑/‑BM25‑/‑conversations‑/‑notes‑/‑settings‑load**.  
- **AWS‑SQS‑/‑Lambda‑drainers** should be **well‑tuned‑in‑memory‑/‑timeout**; use **batch‑sizes‑+‑batch‑processing**.

Right‑sizing can cut **~20–40%** off backend‑costs without affecting UX.

***

### 6. **Keep AI‑inferences as small as possible**

- **Limit‑tokens‑for‑all‑AI‑calls** (chat, content‑generation, formatter, translation, OCR‑post‑processing, TTS‑/‑STT‑inputs).  
- Use **short‑context‑policies** for **voice‑RAG**, **PYQ‑OCR‑summary**, **search‑snippets**, etc.

Smaller‑contexts‑=‑cheaper‑calls‑every‑where.

***

### 7. **Use “free‑credit‑first” rules aggressively**

You already do this well:
- **Each‑specialist‑with‑startup‑credit** (Sarvam‑m, Deepgram, ElevenLabs, Pinecone, Exa, Tavily) **burns‑before‑cloud‑credit**.  
Keep that rule, and **don’t introduce extra‑GCP‑/‑Azure‑AI‑roles** unless you absolutely‑must.

***

### 8. **Kill any “nice‑to‑have” AI‑experiments / features**

- Any **experimental‑AI‑features** (e.g., **auto‑grading‑exams‑with‑Gemini‑RAI‑batch**, **extra‑NLP‑fact‑checks**, **Super‑Robust‑SEO‑scan‑pipeline**) that are **not‑in‑your‑V4‑core‑product** should be **turned‑off** in production.  
- Those can stay **in‑dev‑/‑staging‑only**, not‑in‑the‑100k‑DAU‑bill.

***

### ✅ One‑line cost‑strategy for your browser‑heavy‑traffic

> Since **most visitors are only browsing pages**, minimize cost by **caching‑as‑much‑as‑possible‑at‑Cloudflare‑/‑R2‑/‑D1‑/‑KV**, **enforcing‑30‑AI‑calls‑per‑user‑/‑month**, **pushing‑low‑engagement‑chat‑to‑cheaper‑Workers‑AI‑models**, **tightly‑constraining‑tokens‑in‑all‑AI‑calls**, and **right‑/‑auto‑scaling‑backend‑resources** — so the **bulk‑of‑traffic‑is‑near‑zero‑AI‑cost page‑views** and **only** **high‑engagement‑sessions** pay the **GPT‑4.1‑nano‑price**.
