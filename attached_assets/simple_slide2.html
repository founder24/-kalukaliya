🔄 What happens when you send a message
1
You send a question
Text or voice, in English or Assamese. The app detects which language automatically.
lang ∈ {en, as, hi}
2
The question is turned into a search signal
AI converts your words into a mathematical representation that can be compared to all curriculum topics.
BGE-M3 · 1024 dimensions
3
System finds your exact topic
Matches your question against 197 curriculum topics. Only content from your board, class, and chapter is considered.
Cosine similarity · threshold 0.72
4
Relevant content is fetched
Top 5 most relevant chunks of notes, Q&As, or past questions are pulled from the Knowledge Base.
Top-k=5 · HNSW vector index
5
AI writes your answer using only that content
Also reads your memory — topics you've studied, weak areas, past doubts — to personalise the explanation.
Gemini Flash (EN) · Sarvam 30B (AS/HI)
6
Answer is sent back — and memory is updated
Streamed word-by-word. Optional voice playback. Your study profile is updated for next time.
SSE stream · TTS voice · memory write
🏆 Five things that set this apart
🌐 Bilingual by design
English and Assamese run through the same AI pipeline — no duplicate databases, no separate apps. Hindi is next for national expansion. Built on a multilingual model that supports all 22 Indian languages.
🎯 Can't answer outside your syllabus
Content filters lock every query to the student's board · class · stream · subject · chapter. The AI physically cannot retrieve content from outside that scope — eliminating off-topic hallucinations.
🧠 Remembers every student
Each student has a persistent profile storing topics studied, weak areas, and doubt history. Every answer is personalised — like a tutor who was in every previous session. Works across sessions, months apart.
🎓 School + Degree (NEP 2020)
Starts with SEBA (Class 9–10) and AHSEC (Class 11–12). The same content hierarchy extends to undergraduate degree programmes under NEP 2020 — the first bilingual AI tutor covering school to college.
✅
Human-verified content — every single item
All notes, Q&As, and past questions go through a 6-step staff pipeline: upload → split → translate → AI-index → human review → publish. Nothing goes live without a human signing off. Content quality is a guarantee, not a hope.
