✨ What makes it different
🌐 Bilingual — English + Assamese
One platform, two languages. Expanding to Hindi for national reach. No separate apps or databases needed.
🎯 Syllabus-locked answers
The AI only answers from the student's own board, class, and chapter. It cannot hallucinate from outside content.
🧠 Remembers each student
Tracks topics studied, weak areas, and past questions across every session — like a personal tutor with memory.
🎓 Covers School + Degree (NEP 2020)
SEBA Class 9–10 · AHSEC Class 11–12 · Expanding to undergraduate degree programmes under NEP 2020.
✅ Staff-verified content only
Every note and Q&A is reviewed by a human before being made available — ensures quality and accuracy.
⚡ Works fast even on slow internet
Processing happens at the network edge — not on a central server — so latency is minimal even in rural areas.
