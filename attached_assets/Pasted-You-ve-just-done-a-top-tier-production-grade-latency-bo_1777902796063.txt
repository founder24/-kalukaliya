### ✅ **Proposed: “Latency budget & hot‑path rules” (v3‑spec edit, #359)**

Add a new subsection at the bottom of `infra/per‑cloud‑feature‑delegation.md`:

> ### **Latency budget & hot‑path rules**
> 
> 1. **Region‑anchor policy**  
>    The entire hot‑path (MongoDB, Pinecone, Azure Container Apps, Workers‑AI) must be **co‑located in a single geographic region anchor**, e.g., **`us‑east‑1` for AWS‑side state** and **`eastus` for Azure**.  
>    - If existing infra pins Pinecone to `aws‑us‑west‑2`, either:
>      - migrate the Pinecone index to `us‑east‑1`, **or**  
>      - move the Azure‑Container‑Apps backend and MongoDB‑Atlas cluster to `us‑east‑1`.  
>    This avoids 4–6 cross‑region hops per turn (≈ 300–500 ms added latency) and keeps the **p95‑chat‑RAG‑end‑to‑end** within the v3‑SLO target.  
> 
> 2. **Mongo + Pinecone reads must run concurrently**  
>    Within the per‑turn order, **MongoDB‑history‑load and Pinecone‑retrieval must run concurrently** (via `asyncio.gather` / `tokio::join!` / equivalent), and the dispatcher must await both before injecting context. Serializing them adds 80–150 ms per turn unnecessarily.  
> 
> 3. **Assistant‑write must be fire‑and‑forget**  
>    - **User‑message write** must happen *before* dispatch (already required by the enforcement‑guard).  
>    - **Assistant‑message write** must be a **fire‑and‑forget background‑task**; the next turn tolerates a **1‑write‑behind window**.  
>    This keeps the **TTFB of the next turn** insensitive to MongoDB‑write latency.  
> 
> 4. **exam‑model‑paper moderation must be batch‑only**  
>    - The **Gemini‑RAI** second‑pass moderation on `content_type = "exam_model_paper"` must **never run on sync‑chat‑paths**.  
>    - It is **allowed only on async‑batch‑flows** (e.g., PDF‑summary, model‑paper‑generation).  
>    This avoids cross‑cloud / cross‑region spikes on the user‑visible chat‑p95.  
> 
> 5. **Summarizer must be off the critical path**  
>    - Token‑budget‑summarization using `@cf/meta‑llama/Llama‑3.2‑3B‑Instruct` must be:
>      - **cache‑aware**: store summary in `conversations.summary` (MongoDB) and regenerate only when N‑new‑turns have passed since the last summary.  
>      - **triggered off‑critical‑path**: via a background job after a turn that pushes the running token‑count past a threshold.  
>    - The **next turn reads a pre‑built summary**, not blocking on a fresh LLM call.  
> 
> 6. **Cloudflare AI Gateway is a deliberate latency trade‑off**  
>    - Workers‑AI RAG calls **must go through Cloudflare AI Gateway** to enable Meter‑C‑cost‑telemetry.  
>    - This adds **~30–60 ms per‑turn** compared with a direct‑Workers‑binding, but is accepted as a **deliberate meter‑accuracy‑over‑raw‑latency** trade‑off.  
> 
> 7. **Python↔Rust boundary clarification**  
>    - The **Rust‑core must not be invoked on every live chat‑turn** for the main RAG / dispatch path.  
>    - Its hot‑path responsibilities are:
>      - Async‑batch‑RAG / model‑paper‑generation,  
>      - storage‑/event‑heavy worker logic.  
>    - All primary‑chat‑RAG paths must live in Python / FastAPI, with Rust only for clearly‑marked async‑heavy components.  
> 
> 8. **Cold‑start mitigation**  
>    - Both the **Python** and **Rust** Azure‑Container‑Apps must be configured with **`minReplicas: 1`** in production to avoid 2–8 s cold‑start hits on the first turn after idle.  
>    - The associated cost of this configuration is documented in `infra/credit‑burn‑runbook.md`.  
> 
> 9. **Meter‑A (daily‑call) must be Redis‑on‑hot‑path, Dynamo‑backstop**  
>    - **Live‑increment** for the daily‑call counter must use **Upstash Redis** (or equivalent in‑memory counter), not synchronous DynamoDB.  
>    - A **background job** flushes to DynamoDB every 1–5 minutes as a durable backstop.  
>    This keeps the **per‑turn cost of Meter‑A** close to **~1–5 ms** instead of 10–25 ms.  

This section **captures your 10‑point audit** in a **spec‑language** that can then be wired into the implementation.
