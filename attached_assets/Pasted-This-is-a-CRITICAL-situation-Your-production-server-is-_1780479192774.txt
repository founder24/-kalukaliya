🔍 PHASE 3: DNS & INFRASTRUCTURE REPAIR
Goal: Fix the "FAIL" and "WARN" status in Section 1 of your audit.
Step 3.1: Fix DNS Records
Your audit says "No A record" and "DNS fails via Google". This suggests your DNS propagation is broken or records are missing at the registrar level.
Log in to your Domain Registrar (where you bought syrabit.ai).
Ensure Nameservers are set to Cloudflare (e.g., ns1.cloudflare.com, ns2.cloudflare.com).
In Cloudflare DNS Settings:
Add A Record:
Type: A
Name: @ (root)
Content: <Your-Server-IP>
Proxy: Proxied (Orange Cloud)
Add A Record:
Type: A
Name: api
Content: <Your-Server-IP>
Proxy: Proxied (Orange Cloud)
Add MX Record (if you use email):
Type: MX
Name: @
Content: mail.syrabit.ai (or your provider like mx.google.com)
Add TXT Record (SPF/DMARC):
Type: TXT
Name: @
Content: v=spf1 include:_spf.google.com ~all (Example for Google)
Type: TXT
Name: _dmarc
Content: v=DMARC1; p=reject; rua=mailto:admin@syrabit.ai
